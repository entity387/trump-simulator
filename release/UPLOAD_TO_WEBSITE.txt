TRUMP SIMULATOR RELEASE UPLOAD

After running installer\BUILD_OFFICIAL_INSTALLER.bat, upload:

1. release\TrumpSimulatorSetup.exe
   -> https://trumpsimulator.xyz/downloads/TrumpSimulatorSetup.exe

2. release\update.json
   -> https://trumpsimulator.xyz/update.json

IMPORTANT:
Upload the installer FIRST, then update.json LAST.

Why?
As soon as update.json advertises a newer version, installed games may begin
downloading the installer URL. Uploading the manifest last ensures the new
installer already exists before players are notified.
