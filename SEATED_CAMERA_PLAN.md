# Seated Camera Plan

The player should feel like they are in the chair behind the desk.

## Camera intent
- closer to the desk
- lower than the old front-on room shot
- aimed through the button/desk zone toward the Oval Office background
- desk occupies the lower part of the frame

## Clutter rule
Stage 1 should not look like a command bunker.
Only the core desk tools should be visible at first:
- launch button
- main monitor
- optional desk phone presence
- basic room background

More systems should appear or power on as the stage increases.
