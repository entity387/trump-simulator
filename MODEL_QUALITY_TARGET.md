# Desktop 3D Quality Target

## Camera-built set philosophy
The player should perceive a complete, believable office even though production effort is concentrated inside the gameplay camera frustum.

## Hero assets — highest detail
1. Launch button and housing
2. Desk surface and front silhouette
3. Desk phone
4. Paperwork/folders
5. In-world screens/terminals
6. Fireplace/window zone

## Secondary assets
- lamps
- flags
- framed art
- books
- coffee cup
- pen holder
- side tables
- moulding

## Exterior
Only the view visible through the office window needs high visual credibility.
Use layered physical geometry for parallax rather than building a complete city.

## Visual checklist
- No visibly paper-thin hero objects
- Major edges should have layered/chamfer-like silhouettes
- Materials must differ in roughness and response
- Cool daylight + warm practical lights
- Small props break up perfect symmetry
- Desk remains the visual focal point
- Gameplay information remains diegetic/in-world
