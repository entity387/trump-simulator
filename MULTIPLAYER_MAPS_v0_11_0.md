# v0.11.0 — Real 3D Multiplayer Maps

## Presidential Debate
- Real 3D Trump model at Trump's podium.
- Real 3D Biden model at Biden's podium.
- Real 3D moderator model at the moderator desk.
- 18 real 3D audience NPC models in three seating rows.
- Trump/Biden first-person view is at their standing/podium position.
- Moderator first-person view is at the moderator seat.
- Audience first-person view is at an actual audience seat.
- Podium interrupt controls and moderator mute/next-question controls are separate 3D objects.

## Crisis Room
- Every playable character is Trump.
- Intel Trump, Launch Trump, Radar Trump and Comms Trump are all real 3D character models.
- Each role has a separate 3D station and first-person viewpoint.
- Launch station includes separate LAUNCH and INTERCEPT controls.
- Intel, Radar and Communications have separate monitors and console controls.

## Current scope
This build establishes the real 3D maps, character models and role viewpoints.
Networking and voice chat are intentionally the next multiplayer layer.
